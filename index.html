    Hello! I'm Rashid Iqbal.

    I am an IC Design Engineer with a specialization in Physical Design and Static Timing Analysis (STA). I have 24+ years of experience in the semiconductor industry, including leadership roles at companies like Intel and Cadence.

    With a Master's degree in Electrical Engineering, my career has spanned Asia (Pakistan), Europe (Sweden, Germany, Ireland), and the USA (Colorado). This global experience brings a unique, practical perspective to complex chip design challenges.

    I started this Youtube channel to provide high-quality, practical training on the complex topics of chip design. My mission is to make this knowledge accessible to everyone. This site organizes my 350+ videos into a clear curriculum to help you find exactly what you need.

    My Complete YouTube Curriculum

    My curriculum is structured into four clear tracks. My focus is on Digital IC Design, which makes up ~95% of modern large SoCs/Chiplets. (Analog design is a different, specialized track).

    Track 1: Fundamentals of Digital IC Design

    This track takes you from fundamentals to industry-ready, perfect for students or anyone new to chip design. It covers the core university-level courses, taking you from basic logic gates all the way to a high-level understanding of a complete design flow.

    • Digital Design Basics - Combinational Logic Made Easy (27 Videos)
    • Digital Design Basics - Sequential Logic Explained (8 Videos)
    • CMOS Circuits Made Easy: From Transistor to Logic Gates (28 Videos)
    • RISC-V Architecture Explained - CPU and Peripherals (~40 videos - In progress)
    • Learn Physical Design and Timing Overview - Complete Methodology (~40 Videos)

    Track 2: Physical Design & STA Deep Dives

    This track is my core area of specialization, based on over 20 years of industry experience. Here, we go deep into the advanced, practical topics of Static Timing Analysis (STA) and Physical Design that you'll use every day on the job.

    • Physical Design Flow Explained - Synthesis to Place & Route
    • Full Chip Timing/STA closure - Timing Challenges on Large Dies
    • Static Timing Analysis (Block Level Flow & Methodology)
    • Static Timing Analysis Q&As - Common Pitfalls and Insights
    • Latch Timing Demystified! Real Scenarios Where Latches Shine
    • Timing Constraints Made Easy: Inputs, Outputs, and Reg2Reg Paths
    • Global vs Local Clock Trees — A Practical CTS Guide
    • The Incredible Shrinking Transistor: 350nm to Cutting Edge

    Track 3: Scripting & Automation in Chip Design

    A modern designer is also a programmer. This track covers the essential "glue" skills—Tcl for EDA tools, Python for building simulators, and Vim for efficient editing—that will make you a more effective engineer.

    • TCL Scripting for EDA Tools - Learn the Language of Automation
    • Python for Chip Designers — Build a RISC-V Simulator
    • Vim for Chip Designers — The Essential File Editor
